## Full description (English)

**Think in space — now in light or dark.**

Ultimate Infinite Canvas 3D Pro is a premium hybrid infinite whiteboard where 2D ideas and
real 3D objects live on the same surface. Sketch with a finger or stylus, drop spatial notes,
connect concepts, build a storyboard, import a PDF or place a 3D model — then tilt the camera
and see everything from a new angle.

**LIGHT / DARK / AMOLED**

Choose the porcelain light studio, a deep dark workspace, or true-black AMOLED. The UI rebuilds
instantly, with large readable type and roomy touch targets.

**ONE CANVAS. TWO DIMENSIONS.**
- An infinite GPU-accelerated canvas with smooth multitouch pan and pinch zoom
- Seamless 2D ↔ 3D camera morph without switching documents
- Six-direction navigation, view presets and camera bookmarks
- Handwriting and highlighter with stylus pressure, plus a true 3D ink plane

**IDEAS THAT STAY CONNECTED**
- Spatial notes with live editing, tags, pinning and paper themes
- Smart 3D connectors with curved routing, arrows and labels
- Layers, real parent-child groups, locking and an object hierarchy
- Multi-select, snapping guides, scale and rotate gizmos

**STORY, STRUCTURE AND SPACE**
- Storyboard frames with titles, actions, durations and status
- Timeline strip and presentation playback
- Force-directed idea graph of everything on the board
- Per-board camera bookmarks

**3D WORKSPACE**
- Import GLB and glTF models with automatic fit
- Box, sphere, cylinder, cone, torus and plane primitives
- Real-time PBR material editor: colour, metallic, roughness, glow
- Studio, Midnight, Warm and Neon atmospheres

**ON-DEVICE INTELLIGENCE**
- Smart link suggestions based on TF-IDF similarity
- Cluster and arrange ideas by shared concepts
- Extractive board summary and a practical next-steps list
- Everything runs locally — no uploads and no account

**STORYBOARD TO DELIVERY**
- PNG snapshot, project JSON, Markdown outline, GLB scene and full ZIP backup
- Automatic local autosave and rotating restore points
- Embedded images, PDF pages and 3D models stay inside the project

**PRIVACY-FIRST. ADS OFF WHEN YOU WANT.**
- Your boards stay on your device. No account and no analytics.
- The app is supported by ads: a banner, occasional interstitial and an optional rewarded ad
  that removes ads for 24 hours.
- **Remove Ads — $4.99 one-time** removes banner, interstitial and rewarded ads permanently.
- Everything that used to be Pro — unlimited boards, all templates, all atmospheres and all
  accents — is now included.

**MADE FOR FOCUS**
- Complete Studio Settings: theme, type scale, grid styles, left-handed layout, autosave,
  restore points, data tools, About, Share and Rate.

Your ideas stay on your device.
